#gyro_fchoice: 
Purpose: Controls the gyroscope’s filter bypass and sample rate mode.
0x00: Enables DLPF with 8kHz sample rate.
0x01: Enables DLPF with 1kHz sample rate.
0x02 or 0x03: Bypasses DLPF (no filtering; higher noise, full bandwidth).
 
//////////////////////

#accel_fchoice:
Purpose: Controls the accelerometer’s digital low-pass filter (DLPF) mode.
0: Bypasses DLPF (higher bandwidth, no filtering; sample rate = 1kHz or 4kHz depending on other settings).
1: Enables DLPF (applies low-pass filtering; cutoff set by accel_dlpf_cfg).
